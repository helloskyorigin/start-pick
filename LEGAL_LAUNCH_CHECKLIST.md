# StartPick: Before public launch

This is a **development-only** milestone, not a legal compliance certification. No deployment or live checkout has been performed by this change.

- [ ] Configure an authorized, monitored public support email through `NEXT_PUBLIC_SUPPORT_EMAIL`; do not use made-up addresses.
- [ ] Add the real merchant/operator identity and any required company, address, grievance, billing, or tax disclosures once finalized.
- [ ] Verify actual host/CDN/security logs, retention, cookies and cross-border service providers; update the Privacy and Cookies pages.
- [ ] Before collecting startup ideas, disclose the exact research inputs, AI/search processors, retention, permissions and privacy rights; implement required notices/consent.
- [ ] Before any $29 checkout, disclose actual price and taxes, digital delivery, payment provider, merchant of record, cancellation, refund procedure, customer support, and any mandatory consumer rights.
- [ ] Review legal content for the countries in which the product will actually be offered, including applicable privacy and consumer protection rules.
- [ ] Test all routes, screen sizes, text zoom, keyboard focus, screen readers, and reduced-motion handling on real browsers.
- [ ] Run `npm run typecheck` and `npm run build` successfully and complete QA before considering deployment.

- [ ] Set `NEXT_PUBLIC_SITE_LIVE=true` only after deployment approval; by default search indexing is disabled in development.
- [ ] Verify accessible contrast of all research preview micro-labels against real rendered backgrounds at 320px–1440px.
